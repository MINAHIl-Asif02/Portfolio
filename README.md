## Projects

### 1. Ecommerce Sales Analysis (Power BI)
A multi-page report on sales, profit and loss.
- Tracks total sales, profit, margin, units sold and loss-making orders
- Shows profit by customer segment, region, product category and product
- Compares discount levels with profit to find where discounting leads to losses
- Slicers for year and ship mode

### 2. Airbnb Listings Analysis (Power BI)
A dashboard comparing room types, pricing, minimum stays and listing growth.
- Compares price and supply by room type and guest capacity
- Shows listing trends by year and locations on a map
- Filters by year, city and room type

### 3. Ecommerce Customer Churn Analysis (Power BI)
A retention dashboard that shows who leaves, when, and how much revenue is involved.
- Tracks churn rate, retained customers, revenue and average tenure
- Breaks churn down by tenure group, state, city, payment method and subscription type
- Filters by plan, year and senior flag
